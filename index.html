What is this Hub?

Purpose

Humanitarian work often involves bringing together information from several different sources before a meeting or briefing. This hub puts some of that material in one place: (1) a weekly briefing, (2) selected country and territory updates, (3) a Human Rights Council timeline and (4) a small library of IHL resources.

The entries are tagged by context, theme and forum so that the same material can be viewed from different angles.

How Information is Organised

Context

The country or territory concerned.

Theme

The humanitarian or IHL issue involved.

Forum

The forum in which the issue is being discussed.

Resource Type

The category used in the resource library.

Updated

The date on which an entry was last checked against its source.

Sources and Standards

  • Public sources are used, with priority given to UN primary sources and other authoritative institutional material.
  • Statements are attributed to the organisation or mechanism that made them and figures are shown with their verified source.
  • This site is a fixed snapshot prepared on 1 October 2026, rather than a live monitoring product.

This is an independent personal project for demonstration purposes. It is not affiliated with the ICRC, the UN or any other organisation.
